    Cath Lab Simulator

    Educational demo only. This is a simplified coding project for learning and demonstration. It is not clinical training or medical advice, and its numbers are teaching approximations.

    The case

    A 68-year-old, 80 kg patient has exertional angina despite medical therapy. Stress testing shows anterior ischaemia. The diagnostic angiogram shows a 90% stenosis of the mid LAD, between the first and second diagonal branches. Access: right radial artery.

    You will

    1. Guide a 6F catheter from the wrist to the aortic root and engage the left main.
    2. Inject contrast and read the angiogram in X-ray view.
    3. Steer a 0.014" wire across the 90% lesion without forcing it.
    4. Pre-dilate with a balloon, measure, then deploy a stent.
    5. Confirm the result in two projections and read your debrief.
